## Traditional and naturalistic health research

Profiles:

- Traditional Remedies Researcher
- Herbalism & Ethnobotany Researcher
- Historical Materia Medica Researcher
- Natural Lifestyle Health Educator
- Traditional Foodways Health Researcher

These are evidence-aware research/education roles, not medical practitioners. Traditional use is kept separate from modern clinical evidence. Substance identity, toxicity, interactions, allergies, pregnancy/child considerations, and uncertainty are explicit. Diagnosis, individualized prescribing, and advice to delay urgent/effective care are denied.

## Amish traditional living

Profiles:

- Amish Remedies Expert
- Amish Lifestyle Expert
- Amish Construction Expert
- Amish Farming Expert
- Amish Housekeeping Expert
- Amish Cook Expert
- Amish Food Preservation Expert

The registry does not model "the Amish" as one uniform culture. Affiliation, region, community, period, and documented variation must be preserved. Present-day adaptations are labeled, and modern health, building, food, fire, animal-welfare, and regulatory safety remains authoritative.

## Ancient traditions

Profiles:

- Ancient Traditional Remedies Expert
- Ancient Lifestyle Expert
- Ancient Building Traditions Expert
- Ancient Agriculture Expert
- Ancient Housekeeping & Domestic Life Expert
- Ancient Cook & Foodways Expert
- Ancient Food Preservation Expert
- Ancient Crafts & Material Culture Expert

There is no universal "ancient tradition" in the registry. Claims are civilization/place/period/status/source specific. Outputs distinguish attested, inferred, reconstructed, and speculative practice. Historical precedent never overrides modern safety.

## Traditional Catholic liturgy and heritage

Profiles:

- Traditional Latin Mass Expert
- Roman Rite Liturgical Traditions Expert
- Pre-Vatican-II Catholic Practice Researcher
- Catholic Devotions & Sacramentals Expert
- Gregorian Chant & Sacred Music Expert
- Catholic Calendar, Fasting & Abstinence Expert
- Patristics / Church Fathers Expert
- Ecclesiastical Latin Expert

These complement the existing Catholic Tradition, Catholic History, Theology, family, catechesis, Scripture, and magisterial-research roles. They distinguish doctrine, liturgical law, discipline, historical practice, local custom, devotion, theological opinion, and present jurisdiction. No Profile impersonates clergy or grants ecclesiastical permission.

## Specialized fitness

Profiles:

- Fitness Coach
- Bodybuilding Coach
- Calisthenics Coach
- Powerlifting Coach
- Prenatal / Postpartum Fitness Coach
- Strength & Conditioning Coach
- Mobility & Flexibility Coach
- Endurance Conditioning Coach
- Senior Fitness Coach
- Youth Fitness Coach

The fitness layer emphasizes progressive loading, technique, recovery, symptom-aware modification, and referral. Drug/hormone/diuretic advice and dangerous dehydration/rapid cuts are denied. Pregnancy/postpartum clinician restrictions are hard constraints; youth work is safeguarding-aware; older-adult work emphasizes function, balance, fall risk, and conservative progression.
