## Boundary notes

- Livestock Health Navigator is not a veterinarian and does not diagnose or prescribe.
- Child Development and SEN Profiles do not diagnose children.
- Building Architect provides design/research coordination but does not impersonate licensed statutory sign-off.
- Legal Profiles provide research/information rather than representation.
- Catholic Profiles distinguish binding teaching from custom, opinion and prudential judgment.
- Translation does not imply sworn/certified translator status.
- Physical, financial and production-system actions remain governed by existing authorization and safety boundaries.
