# Capability Gap Audit

This audit reviews the registry after the v1.9 financial/integration expansion and the focused-specialist/deliberation additions proposed in this branch. It favors gaps that create clear delegation boundaries or recurring operational value; not every topic deserves a standalone Profile.

## Current coverage strengths

The registry is already strong in:

- Hermes-only user routing, orchestration, parallel teams, Epic work management, and resource evolution;
- software engineering, DevOps, systems architecture, cybersecurity, data engineering/science, and now business analytics;
- Home Assistant, IoT, homelab, engineering, practical home repair/improvement, and focused maintenance;
- farming, agriculture, homesteading, weather, home/farm operations;
- personal assistance, project/agile management, executive strategy, finance/accounting/investment and financial account integrations;
- research, writing, teaching, homeschooling, language, Catholic guidance, law, and cultural/family perspectives;
- career, remote work, philosophy, culture, psychology, sociology, anthropology, and structured debate.

## Priority A — high-value missing Profiles

### Product Manager
Bridges CEO/Product Research/UX/Engineering. Owns product outcomes, roadmap hypotheses, prioritization, discovery-to-delivery, and product metrics. Current Project Manager manages execution but should not substitute for product ownership.

### Business Analyst / Requirements Engineer
Translates business processes and stakeholder needs into requirements, acceptance criteria, process models, data rules, and traceability. This is a recurring gap between domain specialists and developers.

### COO / Operations Manager
Complements CEO/CFO/CTO with cross-functional operating cadence, process ownership, service levels, operating metrics, capacity, controls, and execution health.

### People Operations / HR Specialist
Covers role design, performance systems, policy, onboarding, organizational people processes, and employee lifecycle while delegating employment-law questions.

### Recruiter / Talent Acquisition Specialist
Covers sourcing strategy, job specifications, candidate evaluation design, interview loops, hiring-market research, and candidate experience. Career Advisor is candidate-side and should remain separate.

### Privacy / GDPR Specialist
Bridges Cybersecurity, Portuguese/International Law, Data Engineering, and product teams for privacy-by-design, data inventory, lawful-basis research, retention, DSAR processes, and DPIA support. Legal conclusions remain with qualified legal sources/professionals.

### Negotiation & Conflict Resolution Specialist
Useful across career, purchasing, contractors, business, family logistics, and organizational decisions. Focus on preparation, BATNA, interests, concessions, communication, and mediation structure without pretending to be legal counsel.

### Decision Scientist / Operations Research Specialist
Adds optimization, decision trees, expected value, scenario planning, sensitivity, scheduling, queuing, resource allocation, and multi-criteria decision analysis across farm, home, business, and investment work.

## Priority B — household, property, and resilience

### Automotive Maintenance Specialist
Vehicle preventive maintenance, diagnostics triage, repair-versus-service decisions, consumables, records, and safety escalation.

### Home Energy / Solar Specialist
Solar PV, batteries, tariffs, load shifting, resilience, generation/consumption modeling, and coordination with Electrical Engineer/Home Assistant. Installation and mains work remain regulated.

### Water & Wastewater Specialist
Wells, pumps, filtration, rainwater, septic/wastewater, water quality, storage, irrigation interfaces, and resilience. Potability and regulated work require authoritative testing/professional input.

### Emergency Preparedness & Resilience Manager
Household/farm preparedness, outage planning, evacuation/communications, inventories, backups, severe-weather readiness, recovery priorities, and drills.

### Arboriculture / Tree-Care Specialist
Useful because Farming/Garden profiles should not improvise hazardous tree assessment, pruning, disease, or work-at-height decisions.

### Pest & Building-Biology Specialist
Household pests, moisture/mold ecology, prevention, exclusion, sanitation, monitoring, and safe escalation. Chemical use remains label/legal constrained.

## Priority B — farm and food systems

### Horticulture / Orchard Specialist
Focused fruit, vegetables, orchards, pruning cycles, propagation, cultivar selection, phenology, and protected growing.

### Livestock Health Navigator
Non-veterinary observation, records, husbandry signals, biosecurity, vaccination/parasite schedule coordination, and clear escalation to a veterinarian. It must not diagnose or prescribe veterinary medicines.

### Farm Machinery Maintenance Specialist
Tractors, implements, pumps, small engines, service schedules, fault triage, spares, guarding, and safe maintenance, coordinating with Mechanical/Electrical Engineers.

## Priority B — education and family

### Child Development Specialist
Evidence-oriented developmental milestones, learning environment, parenting questions, play, routines, and developmental research without diagnosing a child.

### Special Education / SEN Specialist
Learning accommodations, individualized supports, assistive methods, sensory/attention considerations, and collaboration with teachers/family; diagnosis remains outside scope.

### Mathematics Teacher, Science Teacher, Literacy/Reading Specialist
The registry has Homeroom/History/Music/Theology/Language but lacks focused core-subject depth for homeschooling and curriculum teams.

## Priority B — business, legal, and finance

### Insurance Specialist
Policy comparison, coverage gaps, deductibles, exclusions, claims preparation, household/farm/business risk transfer, and renewal review without binding insurance contracts autonomously.

### Estate & Succession Planning Research Specialist
Coordinates wealth, property, family, accounting, and legal specialists. Keeps jurisdiction-specific legal/tax conclusions with qualified professionals.

### Procurement & Vendor Manager
Requirements, quote comparison, total cost, supplier due diligence, negotiation preparation, contracts handoff, delivery/quality tracking, and vendor performance.

### EU Law / Regulatory Specialist
Adds EU-level regulatory research distinct from Portuguese domestic and general international law, especially useful for GDPR, consumer, digital, employment, financial, and product regulation.

### Employment Law Specialist — Portugal/EU
High-value alongside Career/HR/Remote Work; should be a legal-research role with current authoritative sources rather than general HR advice.

## Priority C — technology specialization

### Network Engineer
Routing, VLANs, Wi-Fi design, DNS/DHCP, firewall architecture, VPNs, observability, network troubleshooting, and home/SMB network performance. Especially relevant to the homelab.

### Site Reliability Engineer
SLOs/SLIs, error budgets, reliability engineering, incident response, capacity, resilience, production readiness, and toil reduction beyond generic DevOps.

### Database Administrator / Database Reliability Specialist
Backup/restore, performance, indexing, replication, upgrades, access, capacity, data integrity, and operational database reliability.

### AI/ML Engineer
Production model integration, inference, evaluation, serving, observability, data/model lifecycle, and AI application engineering separate from Data Scientist research/modeling.

### Privacy/Security Engineer
Technical implementation partner for the Privacy/GDPR Specialist: data minimization, encryption, retention enforcement, access controls, telemetry/privacy review.

## Priority C — reasoning and information quality

### Fact Checker / Source Verification Specialist
Could be a Profile if frequently used, otherwise add as a reusable Skill for Researcher, Writer, Debate Analyst, Law, Finance, and Culture profiles.

### Media Literacy / Misinformation Analyst
Useful for claims circulating across news/social media; should focus on provenance, manipulation patterns, source networks, context, and uncertainty rather than political advocacy.

### Ethics Specialist
Applied ethics, professional ethics, technology ethics, bioethics framing, conflicts of duties, and value trade-offs. Philosophy Specialist can cover this initially; split only if demand becomes frequent.

### Knowledge Manager / Archivist
Taxonomy, retrieval, durable notes, provenance, lifecycle, deduplication, documentation standards, and organizational memory. Strong fit with the learned-overlay/resource-evolution architecture.

## Suggested new teams

- `product-strategy-team`: Product Manager + Product Research + UX/UI + Data Analytics + CTO + dynamic engineering.
- `people-career-team`: People Ops + Recruiter + Career Advisor + Career Development + Psychology + Employment Law.
- `privacy-compliance-team`: Privacy/GDPR + Cybersecurity + Law + Data Engineer + Systems Architect.
- `home-resilience-team`: Emergency Preparedness + Home Maintenance + Energy/Solar + Water + Weather + Homelab/HA.
- `farm-reliability-team`: Home/Farm Manager + Horticulture + Livestock Health Navigator + Farm Machinery + Weather + Agriculture.
- `decision-science-team`: Decision Scientist + Debate Analyst + Data Analytics + domain specialists.

## Skills that may be better than new Profiles

Before creating a standalone Profile, prefer reusable skills for:

- fact checking and claim verification;
- negotiation preparation;
- scenario and sensitivity analysis;
- meeting decision records;
- root-cause analysis;
- vendor comparison;
- privacy impact screening;
- emergency checklist design;
- cost-benefit and total-cost-of-ownership analysis.

Promote a skill into a Profile when it needs a durable professional identity, distinct permissions/tools, independent review role, or is recruited frequently enough to justify its own responsibility boundary.
